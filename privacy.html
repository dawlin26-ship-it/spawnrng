Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy covers Spawn RNG Uploader (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Spawn RNG YouTube channel. The App is used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the channel's own videos and Shorts, set their custom thumbnails and schedule when they go public.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm it is signed in to the right channel and to check the status of the channel's own uploads.
  • Google Drive (drive): to read the finished videos, thumbnails and descriptions that the channel owner places in their own Drive folders for upload, and to file them away once they are published.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. The App does not access any other user's account or data.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token files on the owner's computer also removes the App's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the Spawn RNG channel through its YouTube About page.
