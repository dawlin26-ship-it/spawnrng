SPAWN RNG

Spawn RNG is a YouTube channel of 2D-animated comedy documentaries about the weirdest, least-known prehistoric animals, and why it absolutely sucked to be born as one. Every episode of "Why It Sucks to Be Born As a Baby ___" follows one baby animal through its first days: the predators, the disasters and the terrible luck.

The jokes are made up; the science is not. Every fossil, date and study in an episode comes from published research, and the sources are listed in each video's description.

The channel uses a private tool, Spawn RNG Uploader, to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channel.
